## About This Project

This project focuses on improving the online shopping experience of TrendCart, a fictional e-commerce company specializing in fashion and lifestyle products.

The company has identified two major challenges: high mobile cart abandonment and low repeat purchase rates.

As a Business Analyst Intern, the objective is to understand these problems and propose practical improvements.

## Project Objectives

- Simplify the mobile checkout process.
- Introduce personalized product recommendations.
- Provide a guest checkout option.
- Improve customer experience.
- Track business performance through relevant KPIs.

## Business Problems

1. High cart abandonment on mobile devices.
2. Customers face unnecessary steps during checkout.
3. Limited personalized product suggestions.
4. Low customer retention.

## Proposed Solutions

| Problem | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|
| Complicated checkout | Single-page checkout |
| Payment difficulties | Multiple digital payment options |
| Mandatory registration | Guest checkout |
| Irrelevant products | Personalized recommendations |
| Limited performance tracking | Analytics dashboard |
